This folder holds go-to-market, positioning, monetization, and growth-loop docs for Zomzam.

## Files

- [positioning-and-growth.md](positioning-and-growth.md) - audience, wedge, messaging, loops, acquisition channels, and 90-day plan.
- [monetization.md](monetization.md) - free era, Pro tier, CRM/lead monetization, and credit-economy guardrails.

## Strategic Summary

Zomzam should not compete as another generic productivity dashboard. Its strongest story is:

> A social work operating system where builders turn reputation, collaboration, execution, and clients into one connected loop.

The initial market should be narrow:

- Freelancers.
- Solo developers/designers.
- Small service operators.
- Technical creators who already sell expertise or want to.

The launch goal is not broad awareness. It is dense usage inside one or two tight communities where people know each other, post work, help each other, and refer clients.

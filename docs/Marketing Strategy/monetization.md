# Monetization

## Current Recommendation

Keep the early product free until the social and collaboration loops are healthy. Monetization should appear after users already understand the value of profiles, network, CRM, and execution.

## Free Era

Free should include:

- Profile.
- Feed.
- Connections.
- Direct messages.
- Time suite basics.
- Money suite basics.
- Limited CRM usage.

Why: the product needs network density. Charging too early would slow the core loop.

## Future Pro Tier

Best paid wedge: CRM and lead generation.

Possible Pro features:

- Higher lead scraping quotas.
- Bulk lead import.
- Advanced outreach generation.
- Notion/automation sync.
- More project/client analytics.
- Client profitability.
- Saved outreach templates.
- More CRM pipeline views.

Keep social posting, profile, and basic messaging free because they fuel growth.

## Free vs Pro Split

| Area | Free | Pro |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Profile | Full | Full plus advanced proof/portfolio blocks |
| Feed/social | Full | Full |
| Messages | Full basic | Possible advanced search/archive later |
| Time | Core tasks, focus, planning | Automation/reporting later |
| Money | Core accounts/transactions/lending | Advanced reports/client profitability |
| CRM | Limited leads and manual workflow | Higher quotas, automation, outreach, sync |
| Research/outreach | Manual | Bulk tools and provider integrations |

## Credit Economy Guardrails

The old strategy explored Zomzam Credits. If implemented, keep the legal and product line clean:

- Credits should reward contribution and unlock platform actions.
- Do not present credits as cash, investment, yield, or redeemable stored value unless legal infrastructure exists.
- Separate earned credits from purchased credits if top-ups are added.
- Add anti-abuse controls before any marketplace-like behavior.

Good earn events:

- Accepted answer.
- Useful contribution.
- Completing community challenge.
- Verified client win.

Good spend events:

- Boost a help request.
- Unlock extra lead searches.
- Tip another user inside platform rules.
- Access premium AI/outreach actions.

Avoid:

- Cash-out promises.
- Investment language.
- Transferability that resembles unregulated money movement.

## Pricing Direction

Early Pro should be simple:

- Monthly solo plan for CRM/outreach power users.
- Higher tier for agencies/teams only after team features exist.
- Keep one clear upgrade reason: "get more clients and manage delivery better."

## Metering

Good metered surfaces:

- Lead searches.
- Bulk imports.
- AI outreach generations.
- Notion sync volume.
- Stored contact/project limits.

Bad metered surfaces:

- Basic posts.
- Basic messages.
- Profile views.
- Core social discovery.

Those are growth loops and should not be paywalled early.

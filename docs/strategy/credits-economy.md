# Zomzam — Credits Economy ("Zomzam Credits") 🪙

> **Mission anchor:** *Zomzam is the free Life OS for freelancers who don't want to work alone.*
> **The big idea:** Zomzam Credits are an **earned, in-app currency** that lets users access power features (leads, CRM, AI, boosts) **for free — by being active and contributing, instead of paying cash.** Effort is the currency. The more you use the site and add value to the network, the more the site gives back.

> **The non-negotiable boundary:** Credits are **never cashed out.** You cannot redeem credits for real money, and no value ever leaves the platform as cash. Users *may optionally top-up* (buy credits) for convenience — money flowing **in**, spent only on Zomzam's own services, is the standard app-store model and is far lower risk than money flowing **out**. Cash-**out** is the bright line that is never crossed. This keeps Zomzam legally clean (no money-transmitter/stored-value licensing, no KYC/AML) while staying free at its core — earning credits is always a complete, no-payment path to every feature.

---

## 📋 The Model in One Sentence

> *Do meaningful things on Zomzam → earn Credits → spend Credits to unlock the features other apps would charge cash for → optionally gift them to friends.*

Credits replace **money** as the way to access depth — not the way to access *connection* (posting, asking, friends, co-focus stay free regardless of credit balance, always).

## ⬆️ How Users Earn (Generate) Credits

Earning is tied to behaviors that **make Zomzam more alive** — productivity and genuine network contribution. The core two the user defined, plus aligned extensions:

### Primary earn loops
| Action | Why it earns | Earn signal |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Complete tasks** | Rewards the core productivity habit — "more done tasks = more credits." | Per completed task (with daily cap + diminishing returns to stop farming) |
| **Public posts that get reactions** | Rewards *quality* content, not just volume — credits scale with reactions received, not posts written. | Per reaction earned on your public posts (validated by others, not self) |

### Aligned extension loops (recommended, same philosophy)
| Action | Why it earns |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Answer an Ask/Help request** (marked helpful) | The favor economy — helping friends is the heart of the network |
| **Refer a freelancer / share a lead** | Grows the network and feeds the CRM loop |
| **Daily check-in & focus streaks** | Habit reward — small daily drip brings people back |
| **Complete co-focus sessions** | Rewards showing up; reuses existing presence system |
| **Milestone achievements** | "First $1K month," "10 friends helped" — celebratory bonuses |
| **Get vouched / endorsed by friends** | Social proof, peer-validated value |

> **Design rule:** Earning must reflect *real value created*, not activity gamed. **Reactions/help must be validated by other people** (you can't react to your own posts; "helpful" is marked by the asker). This is what keeps the currency meaningful.

## ⬇️ How Users Spend (Trade) Credits

Credits buy the **depth features** — the things that cost real infra/AI money, now accessible for free through effort:

| Spend | What it unlocks |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Leads / CRM** | Unlock additional leads, pipeline capacity, contact slots beyond the free starter amount |
| **AI Outreach** | Claude-powered cold emails — credits cover the real per-use AI cost |
| **In-site purchases** | Cosmetic themes, profile flair, premium badges, custom profile polish |
| **Post boosts** | Extend the reach of a public post or an Ask/Help request to more of the network |
| **Extra capacity** | More co-focus rooms, bigger pods, advanced money tools (multi-currency, exports) |
| **Gift to a friend** | **Send credits to another user** — say thanks, fund a pod-mate, or pay a friend for a favor |

### Peer-to-peer transfer (the favor economy)
Users can **send credits to friends.** This turns Credits into the network's barter currency:
- "Thanks for reviewing my design — here's 50 credits."
- "Take this lead I can't handle, and I'll send you credits for the referral."
- Pod-mates pooling credits toward a shared goal.

> Transfers are user-to-user only, internal, and **carry no cash value** — purely moving in-app utility between friends.

## 💳 Buying Credits — The Top-Up Option

Users who want to move faster can **top-up** (buy credits with real money) instead of earning them. This is **optional convenience, never a requirement** — every feature remains reachable for free through earning. It also becomes Zomzam's clean **pay-as-you-go monetization** (an alternative to a subscription: buy what you need, or earn it free).

> **Why money IN is safe but money OUT is not:** Selling an in-app currency that's only redeemable for *your own* services is **closed-loop prepaid access** — exactly how the App Store, Reddit Coins, Twitch Bits, and game gem-packs work. It is generally *not* money transmission. The moment credits can be **cashed out** to real money, it becomes a regulated stored-value/money-transmitter instrument. So: **buying in = fine; cashing out = never.**

### The Two-Bucket Model (Critical for staying clean)

Because credits are **giftable between friends**, "buyable + freely transferable" could look like a value-transfer network. The fix: track credit **provenance** in two buckets.

| Property | 🟢 Earned Credits | 🟠 Purchased Credits (top-up) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Source** | Tasks, posts/reactions, helping, referrals, streaks | Bought with real money |
| **Spend on first-party features** (AI, CRM, leads, boosts, cosmetics) | ✅ | ✅ |
| **Gift / transfer to a friend** | ✅ (free → low risk) | ❌ **non-transferable** |
| **Cash out to real money** | ❌ never | ❌ never |

**Rules that keep it safe:**
- **Purchased credits are non-transferable and non-cashable** — usable only on Zomzam's own services, only by the buyer. This blocks any buy → gift → cash-out value-transfer chain.
- **Spend purchased credits first** (so the giftable earned balance is preserved for the favor economy), and **log provenance on every ledger entry.**
- **No fixed cash-redeemable peg.** Price packs freely (e.g. bonus credits on larger packs) but never advertise "1 credit = $1 redeemable" — that framing invites stored-value scrutiny.

### New Obligations a Top-Up Introduces

Selling credits is real revenue, so it brings real responsibilities — plan for these before enabling purchases:

| Obligation | Note |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Sales tax / VAT** | Digital-goods tax varies by jurisdiction (most of EU, etc.) — handle at checkout |
| **Payment processing** | Stripe/processor integration, chargeback & fraud handling |
| **App-store cut** | If ever shipped on mobile, Apple/Google take ~15–30% on in-app currency; **web checkout avoids this** |
| **Deferred revenue** | Sold-but-unspent credits are a liability (service owed) — account for it properly |
| **Refunds & expiry** | Consumer-protection rules apply; in some regions prepaid credits **cannot legally expire** — verify before adding any expiry |
| **Fraud / stolen cards** | Buying credits then gifting could launder fraudulent purchases → another reason purchased credits stay **non-transferable** |

> **Bottom line:** A top-up is a legitimate, low-risk monetization path **if and only if** purchased credits are first-party-spend-only, non-transferable, and non-cashable. Keep earning as a full free alternative, and Zomzam stays both monetizable *and* free-at-heart.

## 🛡️ Anti-Abuse & Economy Health (Critical)

An earned currency dies if it's farmable or inflates. These guardrails are **essential, not optional:**

| Risk | Guardrail |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Task farming** (creating fake tasks to earn) | Daily earn caps + diminishing returns; weight by task substance, not count |
| **Post/reaction gaming** (spam posts, reaction rings) | Credits scale with *reactions received from others*, never self-reactions; rate-limit posting rewards; detect reciprocal-reaction rings |
| **Referral fraud** (fake invites) | Credit referrals only when the invited user becomes *genuinely active* (not on signup) |
| **Inflation** (too many credits chasing too little) | Balance **earn rates vs. spend sinks**; tune so credits stay scarce enough to feel valuable |
| **Transfer abuse** (laundering farmed credits) | Transfer limits/cooldowns; monitor abnormal flows between accounts |
| **Hoarding** (credits sit unused) | Healthy sinks (boosts, cosmetics, AI) give constant reasons to spend |

> **The golden rule of the economy:** A credit must always represent **real value created.** The moment credits become easy to farm, they become worthless — and the favor economy collapses. Validate with *other humans* (reactions, "helpful" marks, real activity) wherever possible.

## ⚖️ The Legal Line (Do Not Cross)

| ✅ Safe (closed loop) | ❌ Regulated / Forbidden |
| :--- | :--- |
| Earn credits through activity | **Cash out** credits to a bank/card |
| Spend credits on Zomzam's own services | Redeem credits for **cash** |
| Gift **earned** credits to friends (in-app utility) | Make **purchased** credits transferable or cashable |
| **Buy** credits (top-up) for first-party, non-transferable use | A **promise** that 1 credit = $1 redeemable for money |

**Why:** Money flowing **in** (buying credits redeemable only for your own services) is **closed-loop prepaid** — the standard, low-risk app-store model. Money flowing **out** (cash-out) turns Zomzam into a **money transmitter / stored-value issuer** → state licensing (US MTLs), KYC/AML, tax reporting, and legal liability. **The rule:** buying in is fine when purchased credits are first-party-spend-only and non-transferable; **cashing out is never allowed.**

> *Future note:* redeeming credits for **partner perks in-kind** (a discount on an invoicing tool, a co-working day pass) is *much* safer than cash — but still get any redemption-for-external-value design legally reviewed before launch. Default stance: **internal use + friend transfers only.**

## 🛠️ Implementation Sketch (For When It's Built — Not Now)

A lightweight, future-ready outline (nothing enforced today):

1. **`credits` balance** on the user model (integer, default 0).
2. **`credit_ledger` table** — append-only log of every earn/spend/transfer with `reason`, `amount`, `counterparty_id`, `timestamp`. *Append-only = auditable, fraud-traceable, no silent balance edits.*
3. **Earn hooks** — server-side credit grants on validated events (task completed, reaction received, help marked, referral activated). Never trust the client.
4. **Spend gates** — a single `chargeCredits(user, amount, reason)` helper that atomically checks balance + debits + logs (transaction-safe, mirrors the money-suite rigor).
5. **Transfer endpoint** — atomic debit-sender / credit-receiver with limits + cooldowns + ledger entries on both sides.
6. **Caps & rate-limits** config — daily earn caps, diminishing returns, anti-farm thresholds in one tunable place.
7. **Balance display** — surface credits in the topbar with a clear earn/spend history view (transparency = trust; bridges the Gulf of Evaluation).

> All of this stays **dormant until the team decides to ship it.** Documented now so the design is deliberate and the architecture is ready.

## ✅ Summary Recommendation

1. **Build "Zomzam Credits" as a closed-loop, earned currency** — the free way to unlock paid-tier depth (leads, CRM, AI, boosts) by *using and contributing to* the site instead of paying.
2. **Earn through real value** — completing tasks, posting content that earns reactions, helping friends, referrals, streaks. *More done + more value to the network = more credits.*
3. **Spend on depth + gift to friends** — credits power the favor economy; you can send them to a friend for help or a shared goal.
4. **Guard the economy ruthlessly** — validate earning with other humans, cap farming, balance earn-vs-sinks. A farmable credit is a worthless credit.
5. **Never store, sell, or cash out real money** — closed loop only. This keeps Zomzam free, legal, and mission-true.

> *In Zomzam, you don't pay with your wallet — you pay with your effort, your work, and your contribution to your circle. Credits are the proof.*
